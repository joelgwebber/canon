In most music UIs, playlists are more properly considered part of your library. This could be somewhat complicated by the fact that "playlists", as currently conceived, includes both local and remote playlists. We might consider remote playlists to instead be a special case -- maybe in a new area for import/export affordances, which should be a lot less commonly-used than one's own library.

Let's consider this in light of canon-603e and other yaks in this family, to propose a coherent design.
